# Rice Blast — Resistant Varieties and Nitrogen Management

## Cultural Management
Varieties with resistance to blast: Rasi, Vikas, Krishna Hamsa, Tulasi, IR 64, Aditya, Swarnadhan, Himalaya 1, Himalaya 2, Himalaya 2216, Pant dhan 10, HKR 228 and PNR 519. At the flowering stage, apply nitrogen in small dose, if needed -- heavy or late nitrogen top-dressing favours blast development, the same caution the source gives for bacterial leaf blight (reduce nitrogen application and apply, if needed, only a small dose of N in more split doses).
